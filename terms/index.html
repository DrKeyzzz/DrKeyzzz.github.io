Terms

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Every game is free for every student, always. Students never pay and never see an ad. These terms cover the optional teacher plans that pay for the site.

Plans

  • Starter (free): one class with every teacher feature: assignments, tests, the dashboard, Live questioning, messages from students and the visit log.
  • Teacher ($39 a year, or $4.99 a month): all of the above for as many classes as the teacher needs.
  • School ($249 a year): the Teacher plan for up to 15 teachers at one school, paid by invoice or purchase order.
  • District: every school in a district, priced by quote.
  • Founding teachers: every teacher with an account before teacher plans start gets the Teacher plan free until August 31, 2027, then half price for as long as they keep it.
  • Egg Harbor Township teachers get the Teacher plan free.

Paying, renewing and cancelling

Card payments are handled by Stripe; this site never sees or stores card numbers. Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until cancelled, and a reminder is sent before each yearly renewal. A teacher can cancel at any time from the dashboard; the plan then runs to the end of the period already paid. Schools and districts are invoiced once a year.

Refunds

A full refund is available within 30 days of a payment, for any reason. Write to support@musictheoryacademy.org.

When a plan ends

Nothing is deleted and students notice nothing. The teacher keeps reading every class, grade and test result and can download them. Their first class keeps every feature; their other classes become read-only (no new assignments or tests) until the plan is renewed. Assignments already running finish normally.

Prices and changes

Prices may change, with at least 60 days' notice by email and on this page. A change never affects a period already paid.

Student information

How student information is kept private is in the Privacy Policy. Schools that need a student data privacy agreement can ask for one.
